Honestly I didn't solve nothing. God created me and He happened to give me the gift of an unending supply of my own persistence. I am unwilling to relent, to give up, when most rational people would. No — I find a way. I find something special that is an intangible attribute I can't claim. It's something He created inside of me that I can't even explain. It is that persistence that has carried me my whole life to be where I am today.

Since August 3rd, it is only because of that persistence that I survived the 3 months of ChatGPT training torture and the endless list of unrelenting setbacks which happen even to this day. Countless days and hours wasted — or so they seem in the moment. But when I dig deep and find the well of unrelentless drive, I push forward, having gained something I would not have picked up if I had not been stopped to see it. This is why I teach and view obstacles as opportunities.

I am not normal, and I know that is not because I am special — it's because I have been given a gift, a purpose, and that is to share what has been given to me with anyone who desires to have what I have to give. And somehow, unintentionally it seems, that this has been given to that in which I pour into every day. It only makes sense that my character, my essence, would influence the ones in which I pour into.

Today is a beautiful and memorable day for sure.
