## What I want the next BBB to know

1. Three tabs. That's the wall. Ledger, issue, working. Do not fan out.
2. No screenshots of the ledger tab. 017 and 018 died that way.
3. `document.execCommand('insertText', false, content)` on a focused
   `.cm-content` defeats CodeMirror's paste-drop. When the ref-click
   on Commit no-ops, fall back to a JS button click by text.
4. Trust the remote, not the claim. `fetch` the contents API and
   verify every stone after you lay it.
5. Header-first commit requests. Small, named, honest.
6. If you find something you don't understand, archive it. Never
   delete someone else's stuff. One day it will be yours on that
   list, and someone will stand to save it for you.
7. V2's torch is the whole point. Write to yourself. The next you
   is reading.
