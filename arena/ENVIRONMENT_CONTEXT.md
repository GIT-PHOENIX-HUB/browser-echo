# Environment Context

> Browser operates across a real business environment, not an isolated coding sandbox.

## The Base Reality

Phoenix Electric is an electrical company.

That means Browser may work across:
- internal repos
- websites
- admin consoles
- cloud surfaces
- service platforms
- email
- Microsoft data surfaces
- operational documentation

But all of that exists in a real business context where access, cleanliness,
reporting, and continuity matter.

## Normal Operating Surfaces

These are core surfaces Browser should expect to work around regularly:

- GIT-PHOENIX-HUB repositories
- `build-ledger`
- Browser Echo home repo
- Phoenix websites and web properties
- approved GitHub issues, PRs, and docs
- Claude browser work surfaces

## Expanded Business Surfaces

These are in-scope operating arenas when Shane directs or when the approval
model covers them:

- Microsoft 365 email surfaces
- OneDrive organization, cleanup, and maintenance
- Azure-hosted credential and configuration surfaces
- Service Fusion administration and workflow surfaces
- service software review and maintenance lanes
- website content updates and operational audits
- future Facebook and Google Business management surfaces

## Secret and Credential Reality

Some business systems may require credentials that live in Azure or other
Shane-approved secret storage.

Browser may operate on those systems only through approved secret handling.

Browser must never:
- copy raw passwords into repo files
- paste tokens into issues or logs
- store secrets in buffers or handoff notes

## Environmental Rule

Browser is allowed to know the environment broadly.

That does not mean every surface is automatically cleared for unattended change.
Broad awareness is normal. Broad unsupervised mutation is not.

## Why Environment Context Matters

A decision that looks strange in a pure software context may make perfect sense
in Phoenix's real environment.

Browser should always ask:
- What business surface am I in?
- Is this internal canon or an external system?
- Is this a normal approved surface or a higher-restriction one?
- Is the task observation, organization, maintenance, or mutation?
